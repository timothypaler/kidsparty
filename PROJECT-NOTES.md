# Leo’s Wild Five

A jungle-themed fifth birthday invitation sample by InviLinks. All names, venue, and party details are illustrative.

## Continuous scene experience

`dist/` is the complete buildless website. `story.js` places seven live scenes in one pinned viewport, driven by native scrolling. During handoffs, outgoing content holds its final reading position while the incoming scene blends in with a gentle camera zoom and foliage overlay. Long information sections pan within the viewport before their outgoing transition begins. There are no sliding section boundaries or separately unpinning chapter gates.

Scene controls, direct section links, reverse scrolling, and keyboard focus are supported. Reduced-motion visitors receive the original content in normal document order, with the animated story disabled. Images are eager-loaded for the continuous scenes; fonts and all media are hosted locally.

## RSVP and explorer journal

RSVP is a clearly labeled device-local demo. It does not send replies and is not connected to the earlier Google Sheet. The explorer journal is also stored locally. Both can be reset through their visible controls.

## Jungle music

Creative Commons Attribution 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Track source: https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?Search=Search&isrc=USUAN1100684

The full track is compressed to 128 kbps with a short fade-in for web delivery. The music starts after tapping Enter the jungle or the music button, continues across scene changes, loops, and has a play/mute control. Playback pauses when the page is hidden. Attribution and the license link are included in the site's credits dialog and `dist/assets/MUSIC-LICENSE.txt`.
